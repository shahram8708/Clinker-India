Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Clinker India collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our SaaS platform and related services. By using the Service, you consent to the practices described here.

At a glance

Your data, protected
Encryption in transit & at rest
Tenant-first access controls
Audit-ready activity trails
Clear retention principles
1. Data We Collect
  • Account data: name, work email, role, organization details, and authentication metadata.
  • Operational data: plant, transport, inventory, scenario, and optimization inputs you provide, along with outputs generated for your organization.
  • Usage and telemetry: logs of sign-ins, feature usage, device identifiers, IP address, user agent, and performance metrics to secure and improve the Service.
  • Support data: messages and attachments shared with support, including categories and troubleshooting details.
  • Payment-related references: subscription selections and transaction references; payment card data is handled by integrated payment providers.
2. How We Use Data
  • Provide and operate the Service, including authentication, authorization, optimization, and AI copilot responses.
  • Secure the platform through fraud detection, abuse prevention, auditing, and incident response.
  • Improve reliability and user experience by analyzing aggregated telemetry and feedback.
  • Provide support, billing, and account communications aligned with your subscription.
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce terms, and protect the rights and safety of users and Clinker India.
3. Legal Bases

Where required, we rely on legitimate interest to secure and improve the Service, contract necessity to deliver subscribed features, and consent where explicitly collected (for example, optional communications).

4. Storage, Security, and Retention
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by role and tenant context.
  • Backups and disaster recovery procedures are maintained with periodic testing.
  • Retention is aligned to business need and legal requirements. Upon termination or request, data will be deleted or exported where feasible and lawful.
  • Operational logs may be retained longer for security, fraud prevention, and compliance.
5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for session management, security, and product analytics. You can manage cookies in your browser, but disabling them may affect functionality.

  • Essential cookies: required for login and session continuity.
  • Analytics: used in aggregate to improve performance and usability; no advertising cookies are used.
6. Authentication and Access
  • We encourage MFA and strong passwords. Session controls and CSRF protections are enabled by default.
  • Access is scoped to your organization. Administrators control user roles and access to sensitive features.
  • Login activity and critical actions are logged for auditability.
7. Data Sharing
  • We may share data with trusted processors (such as email or payment providers) to deliver the Service, under contracts that protect confidentiality and security.
  • We do not sell Customer Data. We do not use Customer Data for advertising.
  • Disclosures may occur to comply with law, enforce agreements, or protect safety. We will notify you where legally allowed.
  • Aggregated or de-identified insights may be used to improve reliability without identifying individuals or organizations.
8. International Transfers

Where data is transferred across regions, safeguards such as encryption, least-privilege access, and contractual protections are applied consistent with applicable data protection laws.

9. User Rights
  • You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data subject to organizational policies and legal requirements.
  • Administrators may manage user profiles, roles, and data lifecycle within the tenant.
  • To exercise rights, contact your admin or email privacy@clinker.ind.in.
10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Service or legal requirements. Material updates will be communicated in-product or by email. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@clinker.ind.in or submit through the Contact & Support page.
