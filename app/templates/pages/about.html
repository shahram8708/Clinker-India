Who we are

Built for industrial operators

Clinker India is a professional SaaS platform built in India for industrial operators. We combine optimization science, trusted engineering practices, and enterprise security to keep plants, transport, and inventory resilient.

Technology-led

Modern cloud stack, data models tuned for cement and materials logistics, and AI copilots aligned to operational policies.

Reliability obsessed

SLA-backed availability targets, observability by default, and change controls that respect mission-critical operations.

Partner mindset

We co-design with supply chain leaders, maintenance heads, and finance teams to ensure adoption and measurable ROI.

What Clinker India does
  • AI copilot that answers plant, logistics, and commercial questions in context with auditable references.
  • Scenario planning and optimization engines for production, routing, and inventory buffers with deterministic, stochastic, and robust solvers.
  • Governance controls for access, approvals, and traceability so every decision is logged and reviewable.
  • Billing and subscription controls aligned to multi-plant, multi-entity deployments.
  • Secure integrations with OT/IT sources, telemetry, and finance systems while keeping tenant data isolated.
Why we exist

Industrial operators deserve software that respects uptime, safety, and margins. We are building a product that:

  • Reduces unplanned downtime through predictive insights and disciplined workflows.
  • Balances production, transport, and inventory to protect working capital without risking service.
  • Gives leaders a clear, trusted picture of network health, bottlenecks, and risk exposure.
  • Enables teams to act quickly with confidence, supported by reliable data and defensible AI.

Trust, security, and compliance
  • Tenant isolation with scoped access controls, MFA-friendly authentication, and session protections.
  • Data protection through encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, and audit-ready logs.
  • Operational readiness with backups, disaster recovery playbooks, and observability across services.
  • Privacy aligned to GDPR-style principles: data minimization, purpose limitation, and transparent retention.
  • Change management with staged rollouts, rollbacks, and monitoring to safeguard plant uptime.
How we build
  • API-first architecture with clear contracts for integrations and extensibility.
  • Model-driven planning engines with deterministic, stochastic, and robust options tuned for industrial constraints.
  • Secure telemetry pipelines that prioritize observability while minimizing PII exposure.
  • Continuous validation with synthetic data sets, chaos drills, and performance benchmarks.
  • Human-in-the-loop design to keep final authority with operators and compliance teams.
